==============
ISO 26262 6-10
==============

6-10 Software Integration and Verification
==========================================

10.4.1-7
-------

The certified libraries are tested as laid out in the :doc:`core-certification:safety-plan/testing-plan`, and those test results, for all qualified targets, are available in the :doc:`qualification-report:rustc/index`.

Integration testing is performed as outlined in the scope for the qualified targets in terms of architecture, but final integration is user/integrator responsibility.
